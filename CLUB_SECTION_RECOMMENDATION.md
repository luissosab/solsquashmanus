# Home Club Section Recommendation

## Recommended Direction

Replace the current **four-tile image matrix followed by three detached cards** with one concise section introduction followed by **three matched image-and-copy modules**. On desktop, the modules should form three equal columns; on mobile, each image should sit immediately above its corresponding card.

Each proposition would therefore read as one complete visual unit: **SQUASH** paired with a court image, **RECOVER & TRAIN** paired with either the sauna or cold-plunge image, and **STAY & CONNECT** paired with the general lounge-and-facilities image. The unused recovery image can move to a later recovery-focused section rather than competing here.

This change would solve the section’s main problem: visitors currently have to infer how the three images relate to the three cards. Proximity and a shared region make related information easier to recognize, while a clear scale hierarchy tells visitors what to read first.[1] [2] It also reduces the feeling of “seven separate boxes” without removing any approved copy.

> **Keep:** the mustard section background, exact approved copy, three colored propositions, rounded frames, and real Sol photography. **Change only:** the grouping—one relevant photograph directly connected to one proposition.
